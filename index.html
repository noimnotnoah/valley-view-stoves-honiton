What we do

Complete stove services

From installation through to servicing, everything under one roof.

🪵

Wood & Multifuel Installation

Full supply and fitting of wood-burning and multifuel stoves, including hearths, flues, and building regulation compliance. Every installation signed off to Hetas standards.

🏛️

Fireplace Renovations

Transform an old hearth or open fire into a modern centrepiece. Surrounds, inset stoves, rebated fireplaces — we design it to suit your home.

🔧

Servicing & Maintenance

Annual servicing, flue sweeping, and safety checks to keep your stove running efficiently and cleanly. Recommended every 12 months for peace of mind.

🌀

Chimney Lining & Flue Work

Flexible or rigid flue lining solutions for new installations and existing chimney upgrades. Properly sized and insulated for optimum draw and safety.

Brands we carry

British & European craftsmanship

Steve supplies and installs three of the most respected stove manufacturers in the business.

Each brand is chosen for its build quality, heat output, and distinctive character. We’ll help you pick the right model for your room and budget.

Charnwood Isle of Wight, England

Cast-iron stoves hand-built on the Isle of Wight since 1972. Known for timeless design, clean burn technology, and exceptional build quality.

Clearview Shropshire, England

High-efficiency, clean-burn stoves from the Shropshire hills. Unrivalled burn times and a cult following among off-grid and efficiency-minded owners.

Morso Nibe, Denmark

Danish-designed stoves with a heritage dating to 1853. Morso combines Scandinavian minimalism with serious heat output and enviable efficiency.

About

Steve Turner — Hetas registered since 2007

Steve has been fitting stoves and fireplaces across Devon for 18 years. What started as a passion for real flame and honest craftsmanship grew into a business built on word-of-mouth reputation. Every job —  from a straight swap to a full chimney reline and fireplace renovation —  is done to Hetas standards, with meticulous attention to detail.

Based in Honiton, Steve covers Exeter, Taunton, Sidmouth, and the wider South West. He works with three carefully chosen brands — Charnwood, Clearview, and Morso —  and only recommends a stove he would put in his own home.
