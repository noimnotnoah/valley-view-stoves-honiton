Who

Steve Turner is a qualified Hetas registered installer with 18 years’ experience in the stove and fireplace industry. Based in Honiton, he covers Devon and the surrounding area.

What

  • Wood-burning and multifuel stove installation
  • Fireplace renovations and surrounds
  • Stove servicing and maintenance
  • Chimney lining and flue work

Brands

Steve supplies and installs three of the best British stove manufacturers:

  • Charnwood — Island-honed cast-iron stoves, made on the Isle of Wight.
  • Clearview — High-efficiency, clean-burn stoves from Shropshire.
  • Morso — Danish-designed stoves with a long heritage of quality.

Showroom

Viewing is strictly by appointment. Steve has converted his outhouse into a dedicated showroom with six stoves on display, giving you the chance to see the stoves in a realistic setting and discuss your requirements without the pressure of a high-street sales environment. Call to arrange a time.
